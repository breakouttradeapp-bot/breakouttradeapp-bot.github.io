About the app

Marathi News - मराठी बातम्या is an independent news aggregator. Headlines come from the public Google News RSS feed (Marathi edition); every story shows the original publisher's name and links to the full article on the publisher's own website. The app is not affiliated with any government entity, Google or any news publisher.
